Fares
Travel on the communal shuttle is free of charge for all passengers. No
ticket and no registration are required, and there is no distinction between
residents and visitors. The shuttle is not part of the regional network and
its timetable is published separately by the communal technical service.

Because the shuttle is free, it carries no ticketing equipment and no
passenger counting system. The commune estimates use twice a year through a
manual count over one week, which is why the published passenger figures
should be read as an order of magnitude rather than as a measurement.
